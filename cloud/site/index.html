A little place for you two.

Rucola is a tiny private space for long-distance couples. Leave something for each other, and come back when it is waiting.

nothing to post. nothing to scroll. ↓
1. Pair up

Connect your two phones with a private five-emoji pairing code.

2. Leave something

Send a little message, emoji, photo, or video.

3. Come back

Open Rucola and find the thing your person left for you.
