how it works

Small things. Shared between two people.

No feed, no audience and no pressure to keep a conversation going. Rucola is built around one simple feeling: there is something from your person waiting for you.

1

Pair up

Connect your two phones with your private five-emoji pairing code.

2

Leave something

Send a little message, emoji, photo, video, or whatever feels right.

3

Come back

Open Rucola and find the thing your person left there for you.

nothing to post.
nothing to scroll.

Just the two of you. Rucola is designed for one relationship, not an audience.
Your history stays with you. The phone is the permanent home for your messages and memories.
A little place that feels personal. Simple, playful and deliberately different from another social app.
